# GOS — Market and Positioning

Who else sells gym software in India and Kerala, what makes GOS different, where it falls short, and how to price it.

- **Researched:** October 2026. Most competitor prices come from their own sites or review blogs, so confirm them before quoting to a gym.
- **In short:** GOS is entering a **crowded, cheap market** (₹300–3,500 a month). It can't win on price. It wins on **WhatsApp and UPI built in, no extra hardware, easy switching, and local Kerala support**.

---

## 1. The market

- **Who buys:** small and mid-sized gyms with 50–500 members, run by the owner and 1–3 staff.
- **What they use today:** a paper register, Excel, and the owner's personal WhatsApp. Many have never paid for software.
- **Typical spend once they do buy:** **₹1,000–3,500 a month** for up to 300 members. Low-cost tools go down to ₹300.
- **Kerala:** no Kerala-based gym software company showed up. The vendors targeting Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram serve them remotely from other states.

**The real competitor for most Kerala gyms is "register + Excel + WhatsApp"**, not another app. Those gyms are the easiest to win, and the hardest to make pay.

## 3. What makes GOS different (USP)

Most important first:

| # | USP | What competitors do instead |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Built around WhatsApp from the start.** The owner shares one link in the gym's WhatsApp group; members install and join themselves. | Staff type in every member by hand |
| 2 | **UPI payments with no gateway.** Members pay the gym's UPI in the app, staff tap Confirm. No gateway fee, no KYC, no 2% cut. | A payment gateway with fees, or payments recorded by hand |
| 3 | **Check-in without a biometric machine.** A printed QR poster with an optional location check. | A ₹8,000–20,000 fingerprint device, or a staff-only register |
| 4 | **Quick switch from a register or Excel.** Bulk import understands how gyms write dates ("paid 01/10, valid till 31/10") and calendar months, with a checked preview. | Manual entry, or importing done by the vendor |
| 5 | **Only the features the gym uses.** Classes, PT, workouts and check-in are off until switched on. | Every feature visible, which makes the software feel complicated |
| 6 | **A modern member app** with streaks, progress, a membership pass and the gym's own logo. | Dated designs, or the member app only on expensive plans |
| 7 | **Kerala-local.** Visits, setup and support in person and in Malayalam. | Remote sales and support from other states |

**One-line pitch:**
> *Run your gym from WhatsApp and UPI: members join from one link, pay by UPI, and check in by scanning a poster. No machines, no gateway fees, no data entry.*

## 4. Where competitors are ahead (gaps)

| Gap | Why it matters | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **Enquiry and lead tracking** (walk-ins, trial visits, follow-ups) | Gyms live on converting enquiries | Medium |
| **Automatic WhatsApp reminders** | GOS reminders are one tap but manual; competitors send them on schedule | Medium (needs WhatsApp Business API, see COSTS.md) |
| **GST invoices** | Many gyms need proper tax invoices | Small–medium |
| **Malayalam in the app** | Nobody has it; a clear Kerala advantage | Medium |
| **Diet plans** | Common in Indian tools; members ask for it | Medium |
| **Biometric machine integration** (eSSL, ZKTeco) | Bigger gyms already own one | Large |
| **Staff attendance and salary** | Owners like one place for everything | Medium |
| **Reports** (revenue, renewals, attendance) | Owners want monthly numbers at a glance | Small–medium |
| **WhatsApp or SMS login** | Members without email can't use the app yet | Medium (in the plan) |

**Build first** (biggest effect on selling): enquiry tracking, automatic WhatsApp reminders, GST invoices, Malayalam.

---

## 5. Pricing

Don't compete with ₹300 tools on price; compete on ease, the member app and local support.

| Stage | Offer |
|---|---|
| **Pilot / early gyms** | Free for 2–3 months, with setup and member import done for them |
| **Standard** | **₹799–999/month**, or **₹7,999/year** (2 months free) |
| Included | Unlimited members, member app with the gym's logo, UPI payments, check-in, classes, PT, workouts, import |
| Extra, at cost | WhatsApp offer campaigns (prepaid message credits) |
| Later, premium | Own-branded app on Play Store, multi-branch, biometric integration |

At 20+ gyms, platform costs are about ₹300–600 per gym per month (see [COSTS.md](COSTS.md)), so ₹799–999 leaves a healthy margin.

---

## 6. How to win the first Kerala gyms

1. **Pilot gym:** run it free and import their register for them. Get a short video or quote from the owner.
2. **Show the member side:** members joining from the WhatsApp group in minutes, plus the UPI "I've paid" flow. That's the moment owners say yes.
3. **Lead with "no machine, no gateway fees, no typing"**, not a long feature list.
4. **Visit nearby gyms in person** with a printed poster and a demo phone. Local presence is the advantage remote vendors can't match.
5. **Referral offer:** one free month for each gym that signs up through another gym owner.
